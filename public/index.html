How it works

Living in Lisbon, the words come to you all day. LLYLI helps you keep them.

  1. 1 Capture. Type, say or scan a word or sentence the moment you meet it: multibanco, saudade, tasca. Add where you heard it, if you like.
  2. 2 Get a card. LLYLI adds the translation, a short grammar note and example sentences, so every word comes with its context.
  3. 3 Practise a little each day. Spaced repetition brings each word back just before you would forget it. After each card you say how it went:
  • Forgot
  • Hard
  • Good
  • Easy
